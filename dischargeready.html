01 / The overview

Complex workflows.
Clear next steps.

DischargeReady brings patient context, task ownership, requests and scheduled reviews into a shared desktop workspace. The design supports the practical coordination around a patient’s transition from hospital to home.

Who It’s For

Care coordinators working with ward teams, pharmacists, social workers and caregivers.

Design Scope

Overview, patient details, task board, support requests, scheduling, insights and workspace settings.

02 / The challenge

A clear problem.
A considered response.

Responsibilities and arrangements can sit across separate views. Coordinators need to see what needs attention, who owns the next step and which practical arrangements are still pending.

DischargeReady / A closer look at the interface

03 / Design Details

The details that
shape the experience.

An overview with priorities.

Grouped information and clear hierarchy help important status and upcoming tasks stand out within a detailed screen.

Detail without losing context.

Familiar navigation, headings and actions carry the product structure into more focused records and workflows.

04 / The Design Thinking

Decisions with
a clear purpose.

How the information, interaction and visual decisions respond to the project’s needs.

Start the day with priorities

The overview brings upcoming reviews and workload signals together, with patient-level detail one step away. It establishes a daily starting point without requiring every record to be opened.

Keep patient context connected

Tasks, practical arrangements, episode details and requests sit around one patient record. Clinical clearance remains distinct from practical arrangements, preserving an important workflow difference.

Make ownership visible

Each task carries patient context, an owner, a due time and a status. Required and optional labels add meaning, while filters help coordinators focus on the work that matters now.

Connect requests with the schedule

Equipment, pharmacy, transport and follow-up requests share a queue with priority and ownership. A five-day schedule keeps reviews, caregiver conversations and team syncs in view.

05 / A Consistent Foundation

One experience.
Connected details.

Readable information density, repeated status patterns and consistent navigation connect the dashboard with detailed patient work. Workspace settings group organisation identity, language and timezone.

What The Design Covers

  • Daily coordination overview
  • Patient episode and practical readiness
  • Shared task board
  • Equipment, pharmacy and transport requests
  • Weekly reviews and caregiver conversations
  • Service insights and workspace settings
06 / Design Outcome

A more structured product experience.

A coherent coordination interface that connects people, responsibilities and practical arrangements around the patient.

Readable informationConsistent action patternsReusable product components

Design outcomes describe the delivered experience. Live business performance has not been measured here.
