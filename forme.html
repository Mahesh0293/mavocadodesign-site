FORME

A focused shopping experience for modern fashion.

Industry
Fashion ecommerce
Platform
Responsive website
Scope
Ecommerce UX/UI design
03 / Overview

Let the collection lead.

FORME is a fashion ecommerce experience that balances expressive imagery with a clear shopping structure. The design gives collections and individual products room to stand out while keeping navigation and product information easy to follow.

04 / The challenge

Balance brand expression with shopping clarity.

Fashion imagery needs space to create desire, while shoppers need practical information to consider a product. The challenge was to connect these needs through a consistent journey from collection discovery to product detail.

  • Give product imagery room to work.
  • Create a clear browsing hierarchy.
  • Keep product information and actions easy to locate.
05 / Our approach

A considered path through the collection.

Generous whitespace, a restrained interface and clear typography keep attention on the clothing. Collection browsing and product-detail layouts share a common hierarchy, connecting the visual story with the information shoppers need to make their next choice.

  1. 01Home
  2. 02Men or Women
  3. 03Product listing
  4. 04Product detail
06 / The solution

A clear path through the collection.

01

Discover the collection

The storefront introduces the collection through strong photography and a clear content sequence. Category navigation helps shoppers move from inspiration into browsing.

02

Browse with focus

Product listings use consistent imagery and readable labels to give each item a clear place in the grid. A restrained surrounding interface keeps the collection prominent.

03

Consider the details

The product page brings the image gallery, product information and selection controls into one coherent layout. Multiple views help communicate the garment while maintaining a clear action hierarchy.

07 / Design system

A quiet framework for expressive fashion.

The interface uses restrained colours, thin outline icons and generous spacing to support the photography. Repeated product cards, navigation patterns and selection controls give the storefront a consistent foundation.

  • Product cards
  • category navigation
  • image galleries
  • product selection controls
  • wishlist icons
  • account entry points
  • buttons and responsive grids

From first look to product detail.

09 / Design outcome

A storefront shaped around the collection.

FORME connects a distinctive fashion presentation with a focused ecommerce structure. Consistent browsing patterns and product-detail layouts keep the collection central throughout the website.

  • Product-led presentation
  • Clear browsing hierarchy
  • Consistent shopping layouts
