The product. The people.
Our contribution.

Industry
Fashion ecommerce
Platform
Responsive website
Primary Users
Shoppers looking for everyday western wear and a straightforward way to compare pieces, sizes and fit.
Core Need
Balance editorial expression with a consistent hierarchy for collection discovery and product consideration.

Ecommerce UX/UI, responsive design and art direction
Design Scope
Art direction, ecommerce UI, responsive web design, collection discovery, product details, wishlist, brand story and support.
02 / The Problem Worth Solving

Expressive fashion imagery still needs a clear shopping structure.

A fashion website needs to introduce the collection and give shoppers a useful way to explore it. The brand’s visual character should support the next decision rather than obscure it.

Discovery without direction

Give collection discoverya clear place to begin.

Imagery competing with information

Balance expressive imagerywith practical product detail.

A fragmented brand experience

Connect every pagethrough one visual language.

Design ObjectiveBalance editorial expression with a consistent hierarchy for collection discovery and product consideration.

03 / The Experience We Designed

From first impression to a clearer next step.

01Give the collection a clear first impression.

A clear landing page connects the editorial imagery with collection discovery.

02Keep the story close to the collection.

The about page introduces the brand perspective in a quieter editorial layout.

03Bring product details into focus.

The product view places the item, price and next action in a clear shopping hierarchy.

04Help visitors explore the edit.

The collection grid brings product cards and discovery controls into a consistent browsing view.

05Make the bag state understandable.

The supplied empty-bag screen gives the visitor a clear state and a route back to the collection.

06Give visitors a way to ask.

The contact page pairs common questions with a focused enquiry form.

04 / Decisions Behind The Design

The reasoning behind
the interface.

These are design intentions. The benefits below are not presented as measured outcomes.

01
Need

Make discovery purposeful

Design Decision
Lead with Men and Women, then support the collection with category, size and sort controls.
Intended Benefit
Give shoppers a familiar wayto narrow their selection.
02
Need

Keep product consideration practical

Design Decision
Place fit, fabric, care and delivery information near selection controls in the design scope.
Intended Benefit
Answer the questions behinda product decision.
03
Need

Explain selection and saved states

Design Decision
Use clear size feedback and useful wishlist empty-state copy.
Intended Benefit
Make the next actioneasier to understand.
04
Need

Carry the identity across screens

Design Decision
Rebalance content for desktop and mobile while repeating navigation and action patterns.
Intended Benefit
Keep a familiar experienceas the layout changes.
