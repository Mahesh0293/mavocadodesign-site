What should I include in my enquiry?+

Share what you’re building, who it is for and what you would like to improve. Include a website, prototype, brief or target launch date if you have one. It is fine if the details are still taking shape.

Can we work together remotely?+

Yes. We can collaborate through calls, shared files and focused feedback. We agree on the working rhythm and communication before starting.

Can you improve an existing product?+

Yes. We can review a specific flow, refresh an interface or bring consistency to an existing website or product. Our first conversation helps define a useful scope.

How do pricing and timelines work?+

They depend on the scope, complexity and level of collaboration. After understanding the brief, we outline an approach, deliverables, timeline and fee.

What does a free UX audit cover?+
