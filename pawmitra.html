PawMitra

Everyday animal care, brought closer.

Industry
Animal care
Platform
Mobile app
Scope
UX/UI design
03 / Overview

One place for the care that matters.

PawMitra brings services, health information and everyday care tasks into a welcoming mobile experience for dogs, cats and cows. The interface connects service discovery, booking and animal profiles while giving owners a consistent place to manage care information.

04 / The challenge

Many care needs. One clear experience.

Animal care spans routine appointments, health records, travel and urgent support. The design challenge was to organise these needs without making the app feel crowded, while keeping different animal profiles and their relevant care information easy to navigate.

  • Make care services easy to find.
  • Keep animal records organised and accessible.
  • Give urgent support a visible entry point.
05 / Our approach

Organised around everyday care.

The experience groups related tasks into recognisable service categories and animal profiles. Clear navigation, readable cards and an approachable illustration style connect practical tasks with a warm visual identity. Provider details and ratings support consideration before booking.

06 / The solution

Care that stays connected.

01

Find the right care

Browse relevant services and review provider information before moving into a guided booking flow. Clear cards help owners compare options without losing their place.

02

Keep care history together

The Smart Health Passport organises vaccination and deworming information, medical records, growth details and QR identification around an animal profile.

03

Support beyond the appointment

Travel assistance, the AI Health Assistant and the Emergency Network extend the experience beyond bookings. Cow-specific records accommodate milk yield, breeding and herd information.

07 / Design system

Friendly in character. Clear in use.

Orange accents and a white or warm off-white base support an approachable interface. Animal illustrations add recognition, while consistent cards, outline icons and readable typography keep the experience practical.

  • Service cards
  • provider cards and ratings
  • animal-profile cards
  • health-record rows
  • booking controls
  • bottom navigation
  • form and status states
08 / Experience in motion

From finding care to keeping track.

09 / Design outcome

A connected home for animal care.

PawMitra brings service discovery and ongoing care information into a consistent mobile interface. The design gives everyday tasks a recognisable structure while making room for different animals and care needs.

  • Connected care journeys
  • Organised animal records
  • Approachable mobile interface
