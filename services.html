Before we begin

A few useful
answers.

Can we start with an early idea?

Absolutely. Share the problem, who it affects and what you want to achieve. We can work together to define the right starting point.

Can you improve an existing website or app?

Yes. We can review the current experience and scope improvements to the journeys, content and interface that need attention.

What does the design handoff include?

The agreed scope may include user flows, wireframes, interface designs, interactive prototypes and reusable components, supported by implementation notes.

How do pricing and timelines work?

We begin with your goals, priorities and current stage. The proposal defines the scope, deliverables, timeline and fee before work begins.
