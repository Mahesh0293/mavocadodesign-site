Choices that serve
the task.

Information hierarchy

Identify the active payment method

Keep the selected card prominent with readable last digits and nearby controls. Make the payment source easier to recognise.

Design team
Navigation

Understand progress and completion

Use distinct review and completion feedback rather than relying on decoration. Show which stage the interaction has reached.

Design team
Interaction design

Interpret spending totals

Connect totals to time periods, comparisons and categories. Give spending figures a useful frame of reference.

Design team

Use motion with a purpose

Tie movement to card selection, verification and completion states. Support feedback with motion and clear written information.
