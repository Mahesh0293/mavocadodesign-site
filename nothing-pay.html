The product. The people.
Our contribution.

Industry
Fintech / digital wallet concept

Primary Users
A design hypothesis: Nothing phone owners who make frequent mobile payments, use several cards and want a wallet that fits their device’s visual character.
Core Need
Explore a coherent wallet interface with clear selection, review and completion states.
Mavocado’s Contribution
UI/UX, interaction design and prototype exploration
Design Scope
23 screens and 35 UI states covering Home, Cards, Pay, Insights and Profile. Payment, bank-linking and security interactions are demonstrations.
02 / The Problem Worth Solving

A wallet needs more than a distinctive visual style.

Before a payment action feels clear, people need to know which card is selected, what the next step does and whether the task has finished. This concept explores how a focused interface could communicate those states.

Ambiguous selection

Show the selected cardand its controls clearly.

Uncertain completion

Make review and completionfeel like distinct steps.

Spending without context

Connect spending totalsto a period and categories.

Design ObjectiveExplore a coherent wallet interface with clear selection, review and completion states.

03 / The Experience We Designed

From the selected card to a clear review state.

01Keep the selected card identifiable

Card management presents the selected card, readable details and direct controls in a focused view. These are interface demonstrations rather than live banking actions.

02Make each step visible

The payment exploration shows selection, scanning and review states, with primary actions designed to explain the next step.

03Review the card before adding it

Card entry leads into a review that distinguishes credit and debit types and shows the default-card setting.

04Put spending in context

The insights screens connect a selected period with a spending total, comparison and category breakdown.

04 / Decisions Behind The Design

The reasoning behind
the interface.

These are design intentions. The benefits below are not presented as measured outcomes.

01
Need

Identify the active payment method

Design Decision
Keep the selected card prominent with readable last digits and nearby controls.
Intended Benefit
Make the payment sourceeasier to recognise.
02
Need

Understand progress and completion

Design Decision
Use distinct review and completion feedback rather than relying on decoration.
Intended Benefit
Show which stagethe interaction has reached.
03
Need

Interpret spending totals

Design Decision
Connect totals to time periods, comparisons and categories.
Intended Benefit
Give spending figuresa useful frame of reference.
04
Need

Use motion with a purpose

Design Decision
Tie movement to card selection, verification and completion states.
Intended Benefit
Support feedback with motionand clear written information.
