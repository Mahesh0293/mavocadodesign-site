Nothing Pay

Everyday payments. A little more character.

Industry
Digital payments
Platform
Mobile app and landing page
Scope
App design and interaction design

Independent design concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nothing.

03 / Overview

A fresh take on familiar payments.

Nothing Pay is an independent payment-app concept inspired by Nothing OS. It combines familiar money actions with dot-matrix typography, restrained colour and playful interactions, extending the visual language into a companion landing page.

04 / The challenge

Distinctive design for familiar tasks.

Payments depend on recognisable actions and clear information. The challenge was to bring a strong Nothing-inspired character to the interface while keeping payment controls, account information and recent activity easy to understand.

  • Keep core money actions prominent.
  • Balance dot-matrix expression with readable content.
  • Use motion to support interaction and feedback.
05 / Our approach

Familiar actions with a distinct rhythm.

Pay, Send, Request and Top Up form the primary action group. A restrained account and transaction layout keeps the screen focused. Dot-matrix headings and icons establish character, while Inter supports readable amounts, labels and supporting content.

  1. 01Home
  2. 02Pay or Send
  3. 03Amount and recipient details
  4. 04Review
  5. 05Confirmation
06 / The solution

Familiar payments. Distinctive details.

01

The essentials within reach

Pay, Send, Request and Top Up sit together as recognisable entry points. Account and card information provide context without competing with the main tasks.

02

Activity with less effort to scan

A minimal transaction view gives recent activity a clear reading order. Consistent typography and spacing distinguish amounts, labels and supporting details.

03

Personality in the interaction

Dot-matrix details and playful micro-interactions extend the visual identity beyond static screens. The companion landing page carries the same tone through product mockups and section visuals.

07 / Design system

Dot matrix meets everyday clarity.

Nothing Dot Matrix sets the tone for headings and expressive elements, with Inter handling functional copy. A restrained palette, dot-matrix icons and consistent spacing connect the app with its landing page.

  • Payment action cards
  • account and bank-card visuals
  • transaction rows
  • amount inputs
  • confirmation states
  • dot-matrix icons
  • landing-page CTAs

09 / Design outcome

A coherent payment design concept.

Nothing Pay brings core payment journeys, account information and expressive motion into a consistent design concept. The app and landing page share a recognisable visual language while keeping everyday actions central.

  • Clear payment actions
  • Expressive interaction details
  • Connected app and web identity
