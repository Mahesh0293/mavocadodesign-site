A distinctive digital presence, with clarity at its core.

Industry
Brand and digital presence
Platform
Responsive website
Scope
Website design and art direction
03 / Overview

A presence with purpose.

NOX pairs a confident visual identity with a considered website structure. The design gives content a clear hierarchy and uses composition, typography and imagery to create a cohesive experience across the page.

04 / The challenge

Make an impression. Keep a clear path.

An expressive website needs to remain easy to read and navigate. The challenge was to balance strong art direction with content structure, so the visual character supports the message and visitors can identify where to go next.

  • Establish a recognisable first impression.
  • Make the content hierarchy easy to follow.
  • Keep navigation and actions consistent.
05 / Our approach

Structure gives expression its place.

The page structure establishes a clear reading order before adding visual emphasis. Typography, spacing and image placement guide attention between sections. Repeated navigation and action patterns give the website continuity while allowing individual compositions to carry personality.

06 / The solution

Character, with a clear structure.

01

An introduction with character

The opening composition establishes NOX’s visual tone while keeping its main message prominent. Type scale and image placement create a clear starting point.

02

Content with a clear rhythm

Section transitions, text widths and image proportions organise the story into manageable parts. Each section has a distinct role within the overall page.

03

Consistency across the experience

Navigation, links and action treatments follow a shared visual language, helping visitors recognise how to move through the website.

07 / Design system

One visual language throughout.

The design system carries the existing NOX typography, palette and imagery through repeatable layout and interface patterns. A controlled hierarchy gives expressive content a consistent framework.

  • Header and navigation
  • primary and secondary actions
  • section headings
  • content and image modules
  • link states
  • responsive layout patterns
08 / Experience in motion

See the website in context.

09 / Design outcome

A cohesive digital identity.

NOX brings its content and visual character into a connected website experience. Consistent typography, composition and navigation establish a deliberate presence from the first section to the final action.

  • Confident art direction
  • Deliberate content hierarchy
  • Cohesive responsive layouts
