02 / The Problem Worth Solving

Market information can feel abstract before it feels useful.

Digital-asset information combines unfamiliar concepts, changing figures and a strong visual culture. The design needs to invite exploration while making the limits of illustrative data clear.

Figures without context

Connect each market figureto a useful comparison.

Abstract concepts

Explain abstract conceptsthrough understandable tools.

Unclear functionality

Label sample functionalityapart from live transactions.

Design ObjectiveConnect a distinctive digital identity with understandable sample-data tools and a clear information hierarchy.

03 / The Experience We Designed

From a clear introduction to useful context.

01Give market figures a frame of reference.

The market view groups sample prices, trends and comparisons in a readable interface.

02Explain the offer before the tools.

The introduction connects the website’s promise with a small set of understandable benefits.

03Make learning tools approachable.

The sample calculator and allocation view present educational interactions with clear context.

04Encourage considered habits.

The habits view uses short guidance and a clear sequence to make the reading approachable.

05Keep useful answers within reach.

The questions and closing section connect supporting information with a clear next step.
