05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

What kind of businesses do you work with?+

We work with startups, established businesses and product teams across industries. Whether you’re launching something new or improving an existing product, we start with the challenge you need to solve.

Can you redesign an existing website or app?+

Yes. We review your current experience, identify usability issues and redesign the areas that need improvement.

Do you work with clients outside India?+

Yes. We collaborate remotely with businesses and teams across different locations.

Can I hire Mavocado for just a UX audit?+

Absolutely. Start with an audit to understand what’s working, what’s creating friction and which improvements deserve priority.

How much does a project cost?+

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity and deliverables. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll outline a suitable approach and estimate.
