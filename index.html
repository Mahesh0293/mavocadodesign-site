FAQs

A few things you
might want to know.

What kind of businesses do you work with?+

We work with startups, established businesses and product teams across industries. Whether you're launching something new or improving an existing product, we start with the challenge you need to solve.

Can you redesign an existing website or app?+

Yes. We can review your current experience, identify usability issues and redesign the areas that need improvement.

Do you work with clients outside India?+

Yes. We collaborate remotely with businesses and teams across different locations.

Can I hire Mavocado for just a UX audit?+

Absolutely. You can start with an audit to understand what's working, what's creating friction and which improvements deserve priority.

How much does a project cost?+

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity and deliverables. Once we understand your requirements, we’ll share a proposal with the agreed scope, timeline and fee.
