05 / Design Decisions

Small decisions.
A more considered journey.

The product and task stay the same. The interface changes how information, feedback and actions are presented.

01

Make costs visible.

Version B shows delivery, the sample discount and the final total before the review step. Version A waits until review to show the full breakdown.

02

Help people recover.

Version B puts a specific message beside the email or delivery choice. Version A gives general feedback above the form.

03

Name the next step.

Version B distinguishes Review Demo Order from Confirm Demo Order. Version A uses Continue before review.

This is an illustrative comparison, not a usability study or a measured conversion result.
