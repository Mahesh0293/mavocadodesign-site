A little about us

Good design starts with understanding. The business, the people and the moments that need to work better.

We look beyond the individual screen to understand what the experience needs to do. Then bring the content, journeys and interface into focus.

From an early idea to a growing product, the aim stays the same: create something clear, coherent and useful.

Mahesh ShanbhagFounder & Chief of Fresh Thinking
The person behind the work

Hi, I’m Mahesh.

I work with businesses to turn complex ideas into clear, useful digital experiences. My approach brings business context into design: what needs to be communicated, what people need to do and what could make their next step easier.

At Mavocado, you work directly with the person shaping your project. That means a consistent understanding of the brief, open conversations and care that carries through to the final handoff.

How Mavocado Was Born

Why Mavocado?

The idea came during breakfast with a friend. We were discussing names for our design studio when avocado toast arrived at the table.

“Avocado” became “Mavocado”—a playful name that stayed with us.

We gave the M a meaning: Made for people. It reflects how we approach design—understanding people and making websites and apps easier to use.

A fresh name for a studio built on fresh thinking.
